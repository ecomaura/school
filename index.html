Our Philosophy

Where Little Hands Explore, Create & Shine!

At Little Wings Academy, we believe early childhood is not a race—it is a magical journey of wonder, hands-on exploration, and social bonding. Guided by our motto “Learn • Play • Grow Together”, our curriculum bridges structured Montessori fundamentals with joyful physical and creative expression.

Whether your little one joins us for preschool, after-school activity clubs, or daycare, we provide an encouraging environment where children feel secure, loved, and confident to ask questions.

Early Childhood Program

Preschool — Building Strong Foundations

A joyful foundation from Playgroup to UKG built on Montessori values, phonics, number sense, and sensory discovery.

Curriculum Highlights

Play Group to UKG Educational Journey

We foster natural curiosity through structured play-based modules designed by early learning experts. Your child develops strong phonics, social skills, and creative self-expression.

  • Montessori Based Learning: Hands-on wooden materials, sensory boards, and practical life exercises.
  • Early Literacy & Phonetics: Fun sound associations, storytelling, and early vocabulary building.
  • Number Sense & Logic: Counting games, shape recognition, and spatial awareness.
  • Creative Expression: Sand & clay play, finger painting, music, and dramatic role play.
  • Safe & Hygienic Classrooms: Full AC, CCTV monitored, and child-safe fittings.
